Made for generators and internet in Lebanon

Two businesses, two ways of billing. Sijil handles both — even in the same account, when one subscriber takes both.

Generator subscriptions

Meter bills that work themselves out

Each month, type the kWh price once — for example, when the ministry publishes it. Collectors enter each meter reading on their phone. Sijil works out every bill: fixed fee + kWh used × price.

  • Meter plans and fixed-fee (amperage) plans, in lira or dollar
  • A warning when a reading is below last month or much higher than usual
  • Meter replaced during the month? Enter the old and new readings and the bill stays right
  • A bill waits until both the reading and the month's price are in, so nobody collects a wrong amount
  • Record a deposit or any one-off fee as its own charge

Internet subscriptions

Every package and every renewal in one list

Whether your subscribers pay each month or pay ahead, Sijil shows who is paid, who expires soon and who is behind — and reminds them on WhatsApp.

  • Monthly or prepaid packages, plus bundles (for example 3 months at a set price)
  • Filters for Expiring soon, Expired today and Expired
  • Payment and renewal reminders on WhatsApp, to one subscriber or many at once
  • Tell everyone about an outage, and when service is back, in one message
  • Sell routers and cables, charge for installation, and keep track of your stock

How to start

Start in four steps

We open your account for you and help you set it up.

  1. 1

    Message us on WhatsApp

    Tell us your business name and how many subscribers you have. We open your account and send you your organization code.

  2. 2

    Add your subscribers

    Type them in on the phone, or import your Excel sheet from the web app. Set your plans: meter, fixed fee or internet packages.

  3. 3

    Collect at the door

    Collectors use the Android app, with or without internet, and send each receipt on WhatsApp.

  4. 4

    Check your numbers

    Collectors hand over the cash in the app. You see what was collected, what is still owed and what is left after expenses.

Sijil runs on Android phones and in any computer browser.
