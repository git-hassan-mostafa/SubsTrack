Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Who we are

Sijil is a subscription-billing service for small businesses such as internet providers, generator owners, gyms and delivery companies, mainly in Lebanon. Sijil is run by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS].

2. Two kinds of people

A business that uses Sijil is called an "organization". Its staff use the Sijil app. The organization's own customers may use a read-only customer portal.

The organization owns the data it types in about its customers. For that data, Sijil only stores and processes it on the organization's behalf, as its service provider. If you are a customer of a business and have a question about your data, please ask that business first.

3. What we collect

  • Staff accounts: full name, username and password. Logins use an organization code, a username and a password. We do not ask for or store an email address.
  • Customer data typed in by the business: customer name, phone, address or area, an optional map location link, notes, service plans, bills, payments and cash collected, and meter readings.
  • Error reports: when the app has a problem, it records the error text, technical details, and the username and organization of the signed-in user, so we can fix bugs.
  • Offline copy: the phone app keeps a copy of the organization's data on the device so it works without internet, and syncs it when online.

4. How we use data

  • To run the service: sign-in, customer lists, bills, payments and reports.
  • To show customers their own bills and payments in the customer portal.
  • To find and fix errors.

We do not show ads. We do not sell data. We do not use third-party tracking or analytics.

5. Customer portal

A business can give a customer a link and a password. With them, the customer can see only their own bills and payments. The portal is read-only.

6. WhatsApp messages (optional)

If a business connects WhatsApp, messages such as receipts and reminders to its customers are sent through Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API. Meta then handles those messages under its own terms and privacy policy. If WhatsApp is not connected, no data goes to Meta.

7. Who else handles data

  • Supabase: database and login (EU region).
  • Vercel: web hosting.
  • Expo: app updates.
  • Meta: only if WhatsApp is connected.

8. How long we keep data

We keep data while the account is active. After a deletion request we delete it within 30 days. Database backups roll off within 7 days after that. See how to delete your account.

9. Security

Each organization's data is kept separate from other organizations. We use the access controls of our database provider. No system is perfectly safe, but we work to protect your data.

10. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Staff should ask their manager or organization owner first. You can also contact us at any time.

11. Children

Sijil is made for businesses. It is not meant for children.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.
