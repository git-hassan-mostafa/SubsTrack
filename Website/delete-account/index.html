Delete your Sijil account

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This page is for the Sijil app and website. You do not need to reinstall or open the app to make a request.

If you are the owner of an organization

You can ask Sijil to delete the whole organization and all its data.

  1. Contact Sijil support by WhatsApp [SUPPORT WHATSAPP NUMBER] or email [SUPPORT EMAIL].
  2. Say that you want to delete the organization. Give your organization code and your username.
  3. Sijil confirms the request with you.
  4. Sijil deletes the organization and its data within 30 days.

If you are a staff member

Ask your own manager to remove your account. Admins can remove staff in the Sijil app. Or contact Sijil support as above, and give your organization code and username.

What is deleted

  • Staff accounts: full name, username and password.
  • Customer data: names, phones, addresses, map links, notes, service plans, bills, payments and meter readings.
  • Error reports linked to the username and organization.
  • Customer portal access for the organization's customers.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Database backups keep a copy for up to 7 days after deletion, then roll off.
  • Messages already sent through WhatsApp are held by Meta under its own policy.

The app also keeps an offline copy on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes that copy. This is not needed for the request.

Contact

Contact Sijil support: WhatsApp [SUPPORT WHATSAPP NUMBER] or email [SUPPORT EMAIL].
